# ShiftSwap Project Charter

## Project Purpose

Build a small web app where hourly employees can post shifts they need covered and pick up coworkers' open shifts. A manager approves each request before the swap is final.

## Objectives

- Deliver a focused MVP within one two-week Sprint 1.
- Enable employees to offer and claim open shifts through a clear workflow.
- Give managers the ability to approve or deny swap requests.
- Address operational needs while considering HR oversight and frontline usability.

## Stakeholders & Roles

| Stakeholder | Role |
|---|---|
| Operations Manager | Represents operational needs and prioritizes the workflow. |
| HR Director | Advises on policy, compliance, and appropriate records. |
| Dev Lead | Guides technical approach, estimates, and implementation quality. |
| Frontline employees | Use the app to post and pick up shifts; provide usability feedback. |
| Project Lead | Coordinates Sprint 1, maintains scope, and facilitates decisions. |

## Success Criteria

- An employee can post a shift they need covered and view open shifts.
- A coworker can request to pick up an open shift.
- A manager can approve or deny the request before the swap is finalized.
- Employees can see the current status of their request.
- The end-to-end workflow is demonstrated by the end of Sprint 1.

## High-level Timeline

One two-week Sprint 1: confirm the MVP workflow and acceptance criteria, implement and test the core experience, then demonstrate the completed flow and review follow-up work.

## Assumptions & Constraints

- The MVP is a browser-based web app for hourly employees and managers.
- Manager approval is required; a request is not a completed swap until approved.
- Sprint 1 is limited to posting, claiming, manager decisions, and request status.
- Payroll/overtime calculations and external integrations are not part of this MVP.
- Detailed eligibility and policy rules will be confirmed with Operations and HR; unresolved cases remain subject to manager review.
